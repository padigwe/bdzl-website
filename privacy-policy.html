1. Who we are

BDZL Media provides digital marketing and technology services. We are responsible for deciding how the personal information described in this policy is used. Under UK data protection law, this makes us the data controller.

This policy covers our website and enquiries made directly to us. Where we process information on a client’s behalf, that client’s privacy notice and our agreement with them govern that processing.

Questions about your information?

Email hello@bdzlmedia.com or call +44 7397 912454.

2. Information we collect

  • Enquiry details: your name, email address, selected service and message, plus your phone number and company name if you provide them.
  • Correspondence: information you share when you email, call or discuss a project with us.
  • Technical information: our website and service providers may receive your IP address, browser and device information, request times, pages requested, referring page and page performance measurements when delivering, measuring and protecting the website, fonts or contact form.

Our contact form requires a name, email address, service selection and message so we can understand and answer your enquiry. Phone and company fields are optional. You are not legally required to contact us or provide these details, but we may be unable to respond without enough information.

Please do not include passwords, payment card details or sensitive personal information in your message.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information for the following purposes and legal reasons:

Answering enquiries and discussing work
Our legitimate interests in responding to people who contact us and developing business relationships. If you ask us to take steps towards a contract with you personally, we may instead rely on taking those steps at your request.
Delivering agreed services and managing our relationship
Performance of a contract where you are the contracting party, or our legitimate interests in managing a relationship with the organisation you represent.
Operating and improving the website and preventing misuse
Our legitimate interests in keeping our website available, displaying its content, understanding its performance, delivering messages and protecting our business from spam and abuse.
Meeting legal requirements and handling disputes
Compliance with legal obligations, such as applicable record-keeping duties, and our legitimate interests in establishing or defending legal claims.

Sending an enquiry does not sign you up to a mailing list. If we ask for consent for a separate purpose, we will explain that purpose at the time. You can withdraw consent by contacting us, without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

4. Who receives your information

Information may be available to team members who need it to handle your request and to the providers that support our website and communications:

  • Static Forms: processes contact form submissions, checks them for spam, stores submission records and delivers them to us by email. Spam checks may include automated analysis of message content. See its privacy policy and data processing terms.
  • Google Fonts: supplies the typefaces used on this website. Your browser connects to Google to request the font files, which shares technical information such as your IP address and browser request details. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: supports website delivery and security and provides performance and usage measurements through its Web Analytics beacon. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and its performance beacon information.
  • Hosting, email and IT providers: support the delivery, security and storage of our website and correspondence.
  • Professional advisers or authorities: where disclosure is needed for legal advice, to meet a legal obligation or to protect legal rights.

We limit access and disclosure to what is needed for the relevant purpose. Provider privacy notices explain their own processing; you can still contact us about information collected through this website.

5. International transfers

Our providers may process personal information outside the UK. Static Forms describes processing in the United States, with European Union storage available for some data and Singapore used for email delivery failover. The location of form storage depends on the account configuration. Google and Cloudflare also operate internationally.

Where UK law requires protection for an international transfer, the relevant arrangements must provide it through a UK adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum, as applicable. Contact us for details of the arrangements relevant to your information or to request a copy of applicable safeguards.

6. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry information for the time needed to answer your request, complete any related discussions and manage any resulting business relationship. We decide how long to retain it by considering whether the enquiry is still active, the nature of our relationship, any unresolved issues, and applicable legal, accounting or claims requirements.

Copies may exist in both our email system and the form provider’s records. Provider retention depends on the service and account settings. Static Forms explains its submission, log and backup retention in section 3.5 of its data processing terms. You can ask us about the retention that applies to your enquiry or request deletion, subject to any lawful reason to keep it.

7. Cookies and website tools

Our website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand page performance and usage. Cloudflare states that this beacon does not use cookies or browser storage or identify people across websites. It receives technical and performance information about the current page. See Cloudflare’s explanation of the data collected.

The pages do not currently use advertising cookies. Cloudflare may use essential security cookies if a protection or challenge requires them. Details of those cookies and their lifetimes are in Cloudflare’s cookie information.

Google Fonts loads when you visit a page, and the contact form sends information to Static Forms when you submit it. These connections can share technical information even without a cookie.

If we introduce non-essential cookies or similar tracking technologies, we will explain their purpose and ask for consent where required before they are used. You can also manage or remove cookies through your browser settings.

8. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and the legal basis for processing, UK data protection law gives you the right to:

  • Ask for access to your personal information and a copy of it.
  • Have inaccurate or incomplete information corrected.
  • Request deletion or restriction of use in certain circumstances.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, and object to direct marketing at any time.
  • Receive certain information in a portable format where the right to data portability applies.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it.

Contact hello@bdzlmedia.com to exercise a right. We may need enough information to verify your identity. We normally respond within one month; if a lawful extension applies, we will explain it.

Our website does not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

You can raise a concern with us and also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. You do not have to contact us first.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, loss and misuse, and restrict access to those who need it for the purposes in this policy. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

If a link takes you to another website, that website’s privacy notice applies to its use of your information.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices or legal requirements change and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide an additional notice or request consent before using information for a new purpose.
