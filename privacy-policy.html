1. Who we are

BDZL Media provides digital marketing and technology services. We are responsible for deciding how the personal information described in this policy is used. Under UK data protection law, this makes us the data controller.

This policy covers our website, enquiries made directly to us and our own business prospecting, including outreach to restaurants and hospitality businesses. It applies even if you have not visited our website or contacted us first. Where we process information on a client’s behalf, that client’s privacy notice and our agreement with them govern that processing.

Questions about your information?

Email hello@bdzlmedia.com or call +44 7397 912454.

2. Information we collect

  • Enquiry details: your name, email address, selected service and message, plus your phone number and company name if you provide them.
  • Correspondence: information you share when you email, call or discuss a project with us.
  • Prospecting records: relevant professional contact details, business information, research notes, verification results and communication history, as explained in the business prospecting section.
  • Technical information: our website and service providers may receive your IP address, browser and device information, request times, pages requested, referring page and page performance measurements when delivering, measuring and protecting the website, fonts or contact form.

Our contact form requires a name, email address, service selection and message so we can understand and answer your enquiry. Phone and company fields are optional. You are not legally required to contact us or provide these details, but we may be unable to respond without enough information.

Please do not include passwords, payment card details or sensitive personal information in your message.

3. Business prospecting and outreach

We research restaurants and hospitality businesses that may benefit from BDZL Media’s digital marketing, website and booking measurement services. Where permitted, we contact a relevant professional contact or business inbox by email and may send a limited number of follow-ups.

You can object to our direct marketing at any time, without giving a reason.

Reply to our email asking us to stop, use an unsubscribe link where one is provided, or email hello@bdzlmedia.com. We will stop using your details for direct marketing, including related profiling. We keep a minimal do-not-contact record to help prevent future contact.

Where business contact details come from

  • Public business sources: official restaurant and group websites, including contact and team pages; Google Search and Maps business listings; and publicly accessible business directories and professional business profiles.
  • Contact-finding and verification services, where used: services such as Instantly may supply professional contact details or check whether a published address is valid. A verification result is separate from permission to send marketing.
  • Information supplied directly: details you or your business provide in correspondence, or a relevant professional referral.

We also check public advertising, including Meta Ad Library, and public website and booking pages to understand a business’s marketing setup. Those checks help us assess relevance; they do not involve accessing private advertising accounts or restaurant guests’ booking records.

We record source links and research dates where available. You can ask us which source or provider supplied your details. We use relevant professional information and do not guess email addresses or seek private personal contact details.

What we record and how we use it

Records may include a published contact name and job role, business email address, restaurant or group name, business location, website, business type, source links and dates, email-verification results and relevant public website or advertising observations. For a general inbox, we do not assume it belongs to a named person.

We use these details to assess whether our services are relevant, select an appropriate business contact, personalise our approach, manage emails and responses, avoid duplicate contact and honour objections. Communication records may include messages sent, delivery failures, replies and opt-out requests.

Our legal basis and email eligibility

Where consent is not required, we rely on legitimate interests in identifying relevant business opportunities and promoting our services to appropriate professional contacts. This basis is used only where the purpose, necessity and impact on the contact justify the processing. The interest in growing our business does not override your right to object to direct marketing.

A publicly listed address is not consent. For UK outreach, we distinguish corporate subscribers, such as limited companies and limited liability partnerships, from sole traders and other individual subscribers. We do not send unsolicited marketing emails to individual subscribers unless valid consent or an applicable legal exception permits it. We treat uncertain subscriber status as requiring further checks before sending. Other markets are subject to their applicable rules.

When we obtain personal information indirectly, we provide privacy information within a reasonable period and no later than one month, or sooner at our first communication or an earlier disclosure where required by law. Our marketing emails identify BDZL Media and provide a way to opt out.

Automation and AI assistance

We use automation and AI-assisted tools to help research businesses, organise records, assess business relevance, prepare correspondence and manage responses. This may involve processing professional contact details, research notes and relevant message content through the providers listed below.

This business qualification can determine which businesses we approach and which service we mention. It does not determine your access to credit, employment or other services or produce legal or similarly significant effects. You can ask for a person to review an inaccurate finding or handle a privacy request.

4. How we use your information

We use personal information for the following purposes and legal reasons:

Answering enquiries and discussing work
Our legitimate interests in responding to people who contact us and developing business relationships. If you ask us to take steps towards a contract with you personally, we may instead rely on taking those steps at your request.
Delivering agreed services and managing our relationship
Performance of a contract where you are the contracting party, or our legitimate interests in managing a relationship with the organisation you represent.
Operating and improving the website and preventing misuse
Our legitimate interests in keeping our website available, displaying its content, understanding its performance, delivering messages and protecting our business from spam and abuse.
Researching and approaching prospective business clients
Legitimate interests in relevant business development where permitted, or consent where required, as explained in the business prospecting section. We use minimal suppression records to meet our obligations to respect marketing objections.
Meeting legal requirements and handling disputes
Compliance with legal obligations, such as applicable record-keeping duties, and our legitimate interests in establishing or defending legal claims.

Sending an enquiry does not sign you up to a mailing list. If we ask for consent for a separate purpose, we will explain that purpose at the time. You can withdraw consent by contacting us, without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

5. Who receives your information

Information may be available to team members who need it for the purposes in this policy and to providers that support our website, research, business records and communications:

  • Static Forms: processes contact form submissions, checks them for spam, stores submission records and delivers them to us by email. Spam checks may include automated analysis of message content. See its privacy policy and data processing terms.
  • Google Fonts: supplies the typefaces used on this website. Your browser connects to Google to request the font files, which shares technical information such as your IP address and browser request details. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: supports website delivery and security and provides performance and usage measurements through its Web Analytics beacon. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy and its performance beacon information.
  • Instantly: supports business contact research or verification and, when used for outreach, email campaigns, follow-ups, delivery records, replies and suppression. See Instantly’s privacy policy.
  • InboxKit and connected email providers: support our business email infrastructure and the delivery and handling of correspondence. See InboxKit’s privacy policy.
  • Google services: including Google Sheets for business research, contact records and do-not-contact records, and connected Google email services for correspondence. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Grok: provides AI assistance for business research, organising records, preparing correspondence and handling responses. Relevant professional contact details, research notes or message content may be processed through the service. See Grok’s provider privacy policy.
  • Hosting and other IT providers: support the delivery, security and storage of our website and business information.
  • Professional advisers or authorities: where disclosure is needed for legal advice, to meet a legal obligation or to protect legal rights.

We limit access and disclosure to what is needed for the relevant purpose. The information a provider receives depends on its role. Provider privacy notices and the applicable service terms explain their own processing; you can still contact us about information used for our website, enquiries or outreach.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process personal information outside the UK. Static Forms describes processing in the United States, with European Union storage available for some data and Singapore used for email delivery failover. The location of form storage depends on the account configuration. Google and Cloudflare also operate internationally. Our email, contact-research and AI providers may process business contact details and correspondence in the United States and other countries outside the UK.

Where UK law requires protection for an international transfer, the relevant arrangements must provide it through a UK adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum, as applicable. Contact us for details of the arrangements relevant to your information or to request a copy of applicable safeguards.

7. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry information for the time needed to answer your request, complete any related discussions and manage any resulting business relationship. We decide how long to retain it by considering whether the enquiry is still active, the nature of our relationship, any unresolved issues, and applicable legal, accounting or claims requirements.

Prospecting records: we retain professional contact details while assessing a relevant opportunity, conducting an outreach sequence or managing an ongoing discussion. Retention is determined by whether an opportunity remains active, the last meaningful interaction, the accuracy and relevance of the information, and any legal or dispute-related need. We review records before reuse and after outreach ends, and remove or anonymise personal details that are no longer needed. Minimal contact-history records may remain where necessary to avoid duplicate outreach.

Opt-outs and objections: we retain only enough information to recognise an opted-out contact or business and the scope and date of the request. We keep that record while it is needed to prevent renewed marketing contact and comply with the objection. It is not used to target further marketing, and it may need to remain even when other prospecting information is deleted.

Outreach records may be held in our business spreadsheet, email and campaign tools, as well as relevant research or AI records. A deletion or access request can cover these records; contact us so we can address the relevant systems and providers.

Copies may exist in both our email system and the form provider’s records. Provider retention depends on the service and account settings. Static Forms explains its submission, log and backup retention in section 3.5 of its data processing terms. You can ask us about the retention that applies to your enquiry or request deletion, subject to any lawful reason to keep it.

8. Cookies and website tools

Our website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand page performance and usage. Cloudflare states that this beacon does not use cookies or browser storage or identify people across websites. It receives technical and performance information about the current page. See Cloudflare’s explanation of the data collected.

The pages do not currently use advertising cookies. Cloudflare may use essential security cookies if a protection or challenge requires them. Details of those cookies and their lifetimes are in Cloudflare’s cookie information.

Google Fonts loads when you visit a page, and the contact form sends information to Static Forms when you submit it. These connections can share technical information even without a cookie.

If we introduce non-essential cookies or similar tracking technologies, we will explain their purpose and ask for consent where required before they are used. You can also manage or remove cookies through your browser settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and the legal basis for processing, UK data protection law gives you the right to:

  • Ask for access to your personal information and a copy of it.
  • Have inaccurate or incomplete information corrected.
  • Request deletion or restriction of use in certain circumstances.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, and object to direct marketing at any time.
  • Receive certain information in a portable format where the right to data portability applies.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it.

Contact hello@bdzlmedia.com to exercise a right. We may need enough information to verify your identity. We normally respond within one month; if a lawful extension applies, we will explain it.

The right to object to direct marketing is unconditional. You do not have to explain your objection. It includes any profiling related to that marketing. The opt-out options above apply to both named contacts and general business inboxes.

We do not make decisions about you through our website or business prospecting based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

You can raise a concern with us and also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. You do not have to contact us first.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, loss and misuse, and restrict access to those who need it for the purposes in this policy. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

If a link takes you to another website, that website’s privacy notice applies to its use of your information.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices or legal requirements change and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide an additional notice or request consent before using information for a new purpose.
